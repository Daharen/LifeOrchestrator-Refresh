## Mission

Turn the migration gate's ONE-SHOT A/B charged-retrieval-byte number into a STANDING per-wave measurement.
The i53 gate proved the PCB bootstrap cheaper than legacy by charging retrieval bytes (D-0146 F-i53-eff:
BOOT_PACKET 17,265 B; T-map B 0.51x A; T-prose 0.61x A vs the `B <= 0.7 x A` bar). That number was measured
ONCE, at the gate. Retrieval bytes are the standing cost of every session (the RETRIEVAL PROTOCOL: "RECORD
every open in your ledger -- retrieval is measurement"), so make it a continuous metric: every wave emits a
row recording the orchestrator's actual charged retrieval bytes, so drift is caught the wave it happens
rather than at the next gate. This is the observability half of i55; it directly measures PB-7's bounded-hot
claim over time (`research/2026-08-14-pb7-relayer-design*.md`, acceptance gate G1).
